### Transitive dependencies

Verified on 2026-11-17: two pins in the iPad Package.resolved: GRDB.swift 7.11.1 and NonlocalMatchFilter 1.0.0. GRDB bundles its own copy of the SQLite amalgamation.

## SQLite

SQLite is in the **public domain** (https://sqlite.org/copyright.html). GRDB compiles it in;
no separate license obligations apply.


This iPad preview does not bundle the macOS external HDR/Ansel AI-denoise executables or Ansel weights. The shared PhotonsExtensions resource bundle includes the NonlocalMatchFilter model described below; the mobile interface does not yet expose its denoise workflow. The bundled 43XL calibration is a developer-created user profile.
